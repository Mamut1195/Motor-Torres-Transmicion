# Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Invented engineering rule | Unsafe or misleading output | Block implementation until formula status is source-backed or `TODO_DOMAIN_VALIDATION`. |
| Copied standards text | Legal and licensing risk | Cite sources and summarize decisions; do not reproduce protected text. |
| Plausible wrong numerics | Incorrect analysis despite clean output | Require analytical fixtures, equilibrium checks, and deterministic tolerance policy. |
| Authoritative-looking report | Misuse as final design | Include assumptions, validation status, and `not for final engineering design`. |
| Review-size creep | Reviewer burnout and hidden defects | Keep work units autonomous and under the active review budget. |
